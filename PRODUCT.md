# NextUp
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
android

## Stack
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, CalendarContract, DataStore, WorkManager.

## Users
Personal use on the owner's Pixel 8 Pro. Glance at the next commitments without opening Calendar.

## Product Purpose
A silent, persistent calendar agenda in the notification shade. Full approved behavior is in docs/spec.md.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Local calendar read access, seven-day timed event window, 1–5 events. No custom backend, Google OAuth or event modification. Optional openrouteservice walking estimates add network and precise/background location access with a user-owned key entered locally. Background timing is best effort. Dismissal is intentional and remembered until app open.

## Brand Commitments
Name: NextUp. Clean, minimal Material 3, light/dark Cobalt colors. No navigation bar, onboarding carousel ; walking-time setup is a small native dialog.
