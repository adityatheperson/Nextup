## Global Constraints
- READ_CALENDAR only, notifications and boot permissions; no internet or exact alarms.
- Seven-day lookahead, ongoing events, 1–5 slots, private silent notification.
- Dismissal persisted across reboot and cleared only by app open.
- Background refresh is best effort.

- Midnight/DST and 12/24-hour formatting: localized time tests.
