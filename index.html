What happens to my calendar data?+

NextUp reads calendars synced to your device; it cannot create, edit, or delete your events. Optional routing sends your position and event destination to openrouteservice—not event titles or descriptions. Your routing key is encrypted locally on your phone.

Will it update exactly on time?+

Updates are best effort. NextUp refreshes while open and requests background refreshes at event boundaries, with a periodic fallback. Android can defer them while the phone sleeps or restricts battery use. The progress bar reflects the latest refresh.

Can I dismiss the notification?+

Yes. Swiping it away stops background activity until you reopen NextUp. Dismissal is remembered across a reboot. Public lock-screen content is generic; Android’s privacy settings control whether private event details are revealed.

Why might a walking estimate be missing?+

Walking is optional and needs a valid key, location access, a usable position, and a recognizable physical destination. Online events, unclear locations, routes over 40 minutes, and stale or unavailable estimates are hidden. Live routing and movement behavior still need further real-world verification.

Is NextUp on the Play Store?+

No. This is a personal Android project, currently at version 0.5.0, built and tested on a Pixel 8 Pro. Source and build instructions are available on GitHub. A public packaged APK release is not currently available.
